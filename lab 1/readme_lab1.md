4. Task 3: Conversational Test and General Intelligence
In this task, discuss the difference between a conversational test and
general intelligence.

A conversational test checks whether a machine can respond in a way that
seems human during a conversation. General intelligence is a broader
idea. It involves the ability to learn, understand, reason, and solve
different kinds of problems.

Write your own short comparison and explain why passing a conversation
test does not automatically prove that a machine has general
intelligence.
